# Summitline Alpinist 60L (multi-day pack)

**SKU:** ALP-60-01
**Volume:** 60 L (main) + 4 L (lid) = 64 L usable
**Empty weight:** 1.65 kg
**Torso sizes:** S (40–46 cm), M (46–52 cm), L (52–58 cm)
**Colors:** Slate grey, Alpine green

## Load recommendations
- Max recommended load: 22 kg
- Ideal load window for the suspension system: 12–18 kg
- Sleeping bag straps under the base compartment
- Ice-axe loops (2) and daisy chains on the front panel

## Fit
Sizing is by torso length, not by height. Measure from the C7 vertebra to the iliac crest — S/M/L is a torso range, not a shirt size. Torso length is adjustable within the size (±3 cm) via the ladder-lock back panel.
