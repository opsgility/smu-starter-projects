# Summitline Cirrus 15 (3-season down sleeping bag)

**SKU:** CIR-15-01
**Fill:** 850 fill-power responsibly sourced goose down
**Comfort rating:** -9°C (15°F)
**Limit rating:** -15°C (5°F)
**Regular length:** fits up to 183 cm (6'0")
**Long length:** fits up to 198 cm (6'6")
**Weight:** 950 g (regular)

## Care
- Store LOFTED in the included cotton storage sack — NEVER long-term in the compression stuff sack. Compressed down loses loft.
- Wash only with down-specific detergent (Nikwax Down Wash Direct). Tumble dry LOW with three clean tennis balls to redistribute clumps.
- Never dry-clean — solvents strip the down's natural oils.

## Waterproofing
Shell is DWR-treated ripstop nylon. DWR wears off after ~40 nights; refresh with Nikwax TX.Direct spray-on.
