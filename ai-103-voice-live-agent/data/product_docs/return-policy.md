# Summitline return policy

- **Unused, in original packaging:** 60 days, full refund, receipt not required for members.
- **Used with defect:** Lifetime warranty on manufacturer defects. Bring proof of purchase and photos.
- **Used without defect:** 30 days, store credit only. Field-tested-and-returned is fine — Summitline restocks lightly-used gear to the outlet channel.
- **Custom-fitted items (footwear molded, packs adjusted at fitting):** 14 days.

## What voids a warranty
- Non-DAC pole replacement on the Ridgeline 2 (see tent doc)
- Dry-cleaning down (Cirrus)
- Machine-drying Gore-Tex Pro (Summit Shell)
- Damage from crampons on Trailmark boots (no crampon welt — not rated for it)
