# Summitline Ridgeline 2 (2-person 3-season tent)

**SKU:** RDG-2-01
**Trail weight:** 2.1 kg
**Colors:** Slate grey, Alpine green, Sunset orange (limited)
**Poles:** 2× DAC Featherlite NSL, color-coded — the LONGER (silver-tipped) pole is the ridgepole and goes in FIRST.

## Wind-loaded pitch (>30 km/h)
1. Orient the narrow end (foot) into the wind.
2. Stake all four corners of the footprint FIRST, before raising a pole. This prevents the tent from turning into a sail.
3. Insert the silver-tipped ridgepole; both ends clip into the corner grommets. Hook the fly clips one side at a time — leeward side first.
4. Insert the shorter (gold-tipped) brow pole across the vestibule.
5. Attach and tension the fly. Guy out all six guylines to the pre-tied loops on the fly. On stony ground use the included rock loops instead of stakes.

## Not recommended
- Pitching without staking in >20 km/h wind
- Using non-DAC replacement poles (voids the pole warranty)
- Storing wet — mildew voids the fly warranty
