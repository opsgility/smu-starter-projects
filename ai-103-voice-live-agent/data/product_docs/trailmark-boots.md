# Summitline Trailmark Mid (day-hike boot)

**SKU:** TRM-MD-01
**Weight (pair, men's 9):** 780 g
**Upper:** Suede + Cordura mesh
**Waterproofing:** Summitline BarrierMesh (proprietary — analog to eVent)
**Sole:** Vibram Megagrip
**Sizes:** men's 7–14 (US), women's 5–11 (US)

## Break-in
BarrierMesh softens over ~15 miles of trail. Wear on 3–4 short hikes before a multi-day trip. Blister-prone break-in — carry moleskin.

## Sizing
Runs true to size in North American widths. Half-size UP for a European foot (Summitline's fit is closer to Merrell than to La Sportiva).

## Not recommended
- Do not use for technical mountaineering (no crampon welt)
- Do not machine-wash — hand-wash the Cordura panels with a soft brush; air-dry
