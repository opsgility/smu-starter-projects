# Ridgevault Financial — Q4 Market Brief (House View)

_This is a seed research document. Ridgevault's investment_researcher agent cites
this file by title (`ridgevault-market-brief`) when a client question touches

## House View headline

Ridgevault's Q4 House View is **cautious constructive** on U.S. equities and
**neutral** on developed international. We expect a range-bound quarter with
below-trend growth, sticky-but-cooling core services inflation, and a Fed on
hold through year-end. The rate curve reprices lower in H1 of the following
year, not this one.

## What this means for allocation

For a Ridgevault 60/40 client we are **not** recommending a wholesale change
this quarter. The client's advisor should:

1. Trim the top 3 gainers year-to-date back to policy weight.
2. Add to the **intermediate Treasury** sleeve (7-10y) on any back-up in yields,
   funding from money-market cash.
3. Hold the equity sleeve at policy weight; do not chase quality-growth strength.
4. Rebalance intra-quarter only if any single position drifts more than 4% from
   policy.

## Explicit "do not"s (Ridgevault Compliance-mandated)

- Do NOT recommend individual single-name equities in a client conversation.
  Ridgevault's investment_researcher answers at the asset-class and
  factor-exposure level only.
- Do NOT recommend crypto derivatives, structured notes, or any leveraged ETF.
  Ridgevault's coverage universe excludes them.
- Do NOT frame the House View as a forecast. It is a probability-weighted stance.
