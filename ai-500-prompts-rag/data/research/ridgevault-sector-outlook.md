# Ridgevault Financial — Sector Outlook (House View)

_This is a seed research document. Ridgevault's investment_researcher agent cites
this file by title (`ridgevault-sector-outlook`) when a client question touches
sector rotation, single-sector overweight/underweight requests, or the House
View sector stance._

## House View sector stances

| Sector | House View | Rationale |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Technology | **Neutral** | Post Q3 rally, valuation dispersion inside the sector is now wider than any point in the last 5 years. Overweighting the cap-weighted index is an implicit overweight to 5 stocks. |
| Health Care | **Overweight** | Defensive earnings profile trades at a Ridgevault-model-implied 12% discount to its 10-year median. Positive skew for a range-bound quarter. |
| Financials | **Neutral** | Rate-curve reprice thesis is a next-year story, not this year. Do not front-run the trade. |
| Energy | **Underweight** | Ridgevault's demand model is soft into year-end. We have been underweight since Q2 and are staying underweight. Explicit answer to the recurring "overweight energy for the winter?" client question: **no**, not on House View grounds. |
| Consumer Staples | **Overweight** | Same defensive rationale as Health Care. Pair trade against Consumer Discretionary. |
| Consumer Discretionary | **Underweight** | Real-income headwind + credit-card delinquency uptick. Pair with the Staples overweight. |
| Utilities | **Neutral** | Yield-proxy trade, coupled to the Ridgevault rate call. Wait for the H1 rate move. |
| Real Estate | **Underweight** | Office and regional-bank CRE exposure not yet cleared. |
| Materials | **Neutral** | Coupled to global-growth surprise; no directional edge. |
| Industrials | **Neutral** | Capex-cycle story is intact but priced in. |
| Communication Services | **Neutral** | Reclassified megacap exposure inside the cap-weighted index — implicit overweight risk same as Technology. |

## Explicit sector questions Ridgevault gets and how the House View answers

**"Should I overweight energy for Q4 based on winter demand?"**
No. Ridgevault has been Underweight Energy since Q2 and the House View has not
changed. Cite the demand model, not the winter narrative.

**"Should I overweight tech given the Q3 rally?"**
No. Ridgevault's sector stance is Neutral. Cite valuation dispersion inside the
sector and the implicit 5-stock concentration inside the cap-weighted index.

**"Should I move out of Financials before the rate move?"**
No. The Ridgevault rate call is a next-year story. Do not front-run.

**"Which sector is Ridgevault's highest-conviction Overweight?"**
Health Care, paired with Consumer Staples on the defensive-earnings thesis.
