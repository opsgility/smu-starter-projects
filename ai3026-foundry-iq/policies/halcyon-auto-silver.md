# Halcyon Auto Silver — Policy Summary

**Product code:** HAL-AUTO-SIL
**Effective:** 6-month or 12-month term, renewable
**Underwriter:** Halcyon Insurance Company (fictional)

## Overview

Halcyon Auto Silver is the mid-tier personal auto policy in the Halcyon lineup — sits between Halcyon Auto Basic (state-minimum liability only) and Halcyon Auto Gold (higher limits, more riders). Silver covers liability, collision, comprehensive, uninsured motorist, and medical payments on a single vehicle or a household fleet.

## Coverage limits

- **Bodily injury liability:** $100,000 per person / $300,000 per occurrence.
- **Property damage liability:** $50,000 per occurrence.
- **Collision:** actual cash value of the insured vehicle at time of loss, up to the vehicle's listed value in the policy declarations.
- **Comprehensive (fire, theft, vandalism, glass, animal strike, hail):** actual cash value.
- **Uninsured / underinsured motorist bodily injury:** $100,000 per person / $300,000 per occurrence.
- **Medical payments (MedPay):** $5,000 per person, regardless of fault.

## Deductibles

- **Collision deductible:** $500 per occurrence (default). Optional $250 for a premium uplift; optional $1,000 for a premium discount.
- **Comprehensive deductible:** $250 per occurrence. Full-glass coverage (no deductible on windshield repair only) is included at no additional cost.

## Optional riders (elected at policy issue)

- **Roadside assistance rider:** included at no extra cost with Silver. Covers towing up to 50 miles, jump-start, tire change, lockout service, and up to two service calls per policy year.
- **Rental reimbursement rider:** available as add-on. Reimburses up to $40 per day for up to 30 days for a covered claim.
- **Original-equipment-manufacturer (OEM) parts rider:** available as add-on. Requires that Halcyon-approved repair shops use OEM parts on collision repairs when possible.

## Underlying eligibility requirements

- Vehicle must be a passenger car, light truck, or SUV under 12,000 lbs GVWR.
- Vehicle must be titled to the named insured or a household member.
- Insured must have a valid driver's license in the state of residence and no more than two moving violations in the past three years.
- Vehicles over 15 model years old require a Halcyon underwriter inspection.

## Standard exclusions

- Use of the vehicle as a taxi, rideshare, or delivery vehicle (Halcyon Rideshare Rider is a separate product).
- Racing, timed speed events, or off-road use.
- Damage from mechanical or electrical breakdown (Halcyon does not offer a mechanical breakdown rider).
- Intentional damage caused by the insured.
- Damage from a driver operating the vehicle without permission.

## Waiting periods and effective dates

- Coverage is effective from the policy start date and time.
- No waiting period for collision, comprehensive, or liability coverages.
- Roadside assistance is immediately available.

## Claim-reporting requirements

- Notice of a claim must be reported to Halcyon within 30 days of the accident or discovery of loss.
- Photographs, police report (where applicable), and other-party contact info are required before Halcyon will assign an adjuster.

## Renewal and premium notes

- Premium is calculated on driver age band, driving record, garaging ZIP code, vehicle class, annual mileage, and prior claim history.
- Halcyon does not surcharge for a single not-at-fault claim in the past three years. Two or more not-at-fault claims may trigger a surcharge on the next renewal.
