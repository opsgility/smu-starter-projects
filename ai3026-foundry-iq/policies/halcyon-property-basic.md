# Halcyon Property Basic — Policy Summary

**Product code:** HAL-PROP-BAS
**Effective:** 12-month term, renewable
**Underwriter:** Halcyon Insurance Company (fictional)

## Overview

Halcyon Property Basic is the entry-tier homeowners policy in the Halcyon lineup — designed for single-family residences under $400,000 replacement cost, with straightforward coverages and a shorter list of optional riders than Halcyon Property Premium.

## Coverage limits

- **Dwelling (Coverage A):** up to $250,000 actual cash value at time of loss.
- **Personal property (Coverage C):** up to $100,000 replacement cost for scheduled items, actual cash value otherwise.
- **Loss of use (Coverage D):** up to $40,000 in additional living expenses for up to 24 months.
- **Personal liability (Coverage E):** $100,000 per occurrence, $100,000 aggregate.
- **Medical payments to others (Coverage F):** $1,000 per person.
- **Other structures (Coverage B):** 10% of dwelling coverage, i.e. up to $25,000.

## Deductibles

- **All-perils deductible:** $1,500 per occurrence (default). Optional $500 for premium uplift; optional $2,500 for premium discount.
- **Named-windstorm and hail deductible:** 2% of Coverage A limit ($5,000 on a $250,000 dwelling), applied to storm-related property losses in Atlantic and Gulf coastal counties.

## Covered perils (named-perils basis)

- Fire and lightning
- Windstorm and hail (subject to named-storm deductible where noted)
- Explosion
- Riot or civil commotion
- Damage from aircraft or vehicles
- Smoke
- Vandalism and malicious mischief
- Theft
- Volcanic eruption
- Sudden accidental water discharge from a plumbing or heating system (see exclusions for flood and slow-leak losses)

## Standard exclusions

- **Flood** — losses from surface water, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water. Halcyon does NOT offer a flood rider; the National Flood Insurance Program is the required source.
- **Earthquake and earth movement** — separately purchasable via Halcyon Earthquake Endorsement (not included in Basic).
- **Wear, tear, deterioration, and gradual water leaks** — losses that develop over 14+ days are considered maintenance, not sudden accidental discharge.
- **Mold** except when directly caused by a covered sudden water discharge, and then capped at $10,000.
- **Sewer or drain backup** — separately purchasable via Halcyon Water Backup Endorsement (not included in Basic).
- **Business use** of the residence beyond incidental home-office use.
- **Intentional acts** by the insured.

## Optional riders (elected at policy issue)

Halcyon Property Basic is intentionally lean on riders. The following are available:

- **Scheduled personal property rider:** for high-value items (jewelry, art, collectibles) that exceed the $2,500 sub-limit for those categories. Requires appraisals.
- **Home business rider:** extends liability and property coverage to a home-based business with fewer than 3 non-family employees.

Halcyon Property Basic does NOT support water damage, earthquake, or identity-theft riders. Customers who need those coverages should quote Halcyon Property Premium.

## Waiting periods and effective dates

- Coverage is effective from the policy start date and time.
- The named-windstorm deductible is enforced only for storms named by NOAA at least 72 hours before landfall in the insured's county.

## Claim-reporting requirements

- Notice of a claim must be provided to Halcyon within 45 days of the loss or discovery.
- Photographs, receipts for damaged property, and (where applicable) police reports are required.

## Renewal and premium notes

- Premium is calculated on dwelling replacement cost, construction type, roof age, distance to fire hydrant / fire station, prior-claim history, and credit tier.
- Halcyon may non-renew the policy for two or more claims in a rolling three-year window.
