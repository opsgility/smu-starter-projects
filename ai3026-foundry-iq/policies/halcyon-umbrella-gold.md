# Halcyon Umbrella Gold — Policy Summary

**Product code:** HAL-UMB-GOLD
**Effective:** rolling 12-month term, renewable
**Underwriter:** Halcyon Insurance Company (fictional)

## Overview

Halcyon Umbrella Gold is a mid-tier personal umbrella policy that layers over an insured's underlying auto and homeowners policies. It extends bodily-injury and property-damage liability protection beyond the limits of the underlying policies and adds coverage for a limited set of gaps the underlying policies exclude.

## Coverage limits

- **Third-party bodily injury liability:** up to $2,000,000 per occurrence, $2,000,000 aggregate per policy year.
- **Third-party property damage liability:** up to $2,000,000 per occurrence, $2,000,000 aggregate.
- **Personal injury (defamation, false arrest, invasion of privacy):** up to $250,000 per claim.
- **Uninsured / underinsured motorist bodily injury:** matches the underlying auto policy limit up to $2,000,000.
- **Legal defense costs:** covered in addition to the coverage limits above, not netted against them.

## Deductible

- **Standard deductible:** $2,500 per claim, applied only when the underlying policy does not respond.
- No deductible applies when the umbrella is triggered strictly to extend limits above the underlying policy.

## Underlying policy requirements

- **Underlying auto liability:** minimum $250,000 per person / $500,000 per occurrence bodily injury, $100,000 property damage.
- **Underlying homeowners liability:** minimum $300,000 per occurrence.
- Coverage lapses on the underlying policy pause umbrella coverage from the lapse date until the underlying policy is reinstated.

## Optional riders (elected at policy issue)

- **Water damage rider:** extends coverage for sudden and accidental water discharge (burst pipes, appliance overflow). Adds $50,000 per occurrence over the underlying homeowners.
- **Identity theft rider:** reimburses out-of-pocket costs to restore identity following theft, up to $50,000 per year. Does not cover direct loss of funds.

## Standard exclusions

- Intentional acts by the insured.
- Business, professional, or commercial pursuits (Halcyon offers a separate Business Umbrella product).
- Motorized watercraft over 26 feet or exceeding 50 mph.
- Owned or operated aircraft.
- Communicable disease transmission (added exclusion effective 2022 and retained through subsequent renewals).
- Claims arising from acts of war or nuclear hazards.

## Waiting periods

- **New-policy waiting period:** none for occurrences after the effective date.
- **Rider waiting periods:** water damage rider has a 14-day waiting period from rider add-date; identity theft rider is immediate.

## Claim-reporting requirements

- Notice of a claim or potential claim must be provided to Halcyon within 30 days of the insured becoming aware of the underlying event.
- Late notice (beyond 60 days) may reduce or void coverage if it prejudices Halcyon's investigation or defense.

## Renewal and premium notes

- Renewals are annual and Halcyon issues renewal notices at least 45 days before the term end.
- Premium is set based on the underlying auto and homeowners limits, insured age band, credit tier, and prior-claim history. Halcyon does not surcharge for a single not-at-fault umbrella claim in the last three years.
