# Halcyon Umbrella Platinum — Policy Summary

**Product code:** HAL-UMB-PLAT
**Effective:** 12-month term, renewable
**Underwriter:** Halcyon Insurance Company (fictional)

## Overview

Halcyon Umbrella Platinum is the top-tier personal umbrella policy in the Halcyon lineup. It layers over an insured's underlying auto, homeowners, and (where applicable) recreational vehicle policies with substantially higher limits than Halcyon Umbrella Gold, and adds coverage for high-net-worth exposures — art collections, cyber-liability, and identity restoration.

## Coverage limits

- **Third-party bodily injury liability:** up to $5,000,000 per occurrence, $5,000,000 aggregate per policy year.
- **Third-party property damage liability:** up to $5,000,000 per occurrence, $5,000,000 aggregate.
- **Personal injury (defamation, false arrest, invasion of privacy):** up to $1,000,000 per claim.
- **Uninsured / underinsured motorist bodily injury:** matches the underlying auto policy limit up to $5,000,000.
- **Legal defense costs:** covered in addition to the coverage limits above, not netted against them.
- **Worldwide coverage:** included at no extra premium for occurrences outside the United States.

## Deductible

- **Standard deductible:** $5,000 per claim, applied only when the underlying policy does not respond.
- No deductible applies when the umbrella is triggered strictly to extend limits above the underlying policy.

## Underlying policy requirements

- **Underlying auto liability:** minimum $500,000 per person / $1,000,000 per occurrence bodily injury, $250,000 property damage.
- **Underlying homeowners liability:** minimum $500,000 per occurrence.
- **Watercraft and recreational vehicle policies:** minimum $500,000 liability where applicable.

## Included riders (bundled with Platinum, no separate election required)

- **Water damage rider:** covers sudden and accidental water discharge (burst pipes, appliance overflow, ice dam damage). Adds $250,000 per occurrence over underlying homeowners.
- **Identity theft rider:** reimburses restoration costs and lost wages up to $100,000 per year following identity theft, includes 24/7 access to a fraud-specialist case manager.
- **Cyber-liability rider:** covers first-party losses (ransomware, extortion, data-restoration costs) up to $250,000 and third-party liability from a data breach up to $500,000. Does NOT cover business-use exposures.
- **Art collection rider:** scheduled fine-art and collectibles coverage up to $500,000 aggregate. Requires appraisals within the last 5 years for items over $10,000 individual value.

## Standard exclusions

- Intentional acts by the insured.
- Business, professional, or commercial pursuits (Halcyon offers a separate Business Umbrella product).
- Motorized watercraft over 100 feet or exceeding 65 mph.
- Owned or operated aircraft other than model / hobby drones under 5 lbs.
- Communicable disease transmission (added exclusion effective 2022 and retained through subsequent renewals).
- Claims arising from acts of war or nuclear hazards.
- Cryptocurrency losses (added exclusion effective 2024).

## Waiting periods

- **New-policy waiting period:** none for occurrences after the effective date.
- **Cyber rider waiting period:** 7 days from rider effective date.
- **Art collection rider waiting period:** none if pre-issue appraisals are on file; 30 days otherwise.

## Claim-reporting requirements

- Notice of a claim or potential claim must be provided to Halcyon within 30 days of the insured becoming aware of the underlying event.
- Cyber events require notice within 72 hours to preserve full coverage.
- Late notice (beyond 60 days) may reduce or void coverage if it prejudices Halcyon's investigation or defense.

## Renewal and premium notes

- Renewals are annual and Halcyon issues renewal notices at least 45 days before term end.
- Halcyon Platinum insureds get a dedicated account manager for renewal reviews and cross-policy coordination.
- Premium is calculated on underlying limits, insured net-worth band, credit tier, and prior-claim history. Halcyon does not surcharge for a single not-at-fault umbrella claim in the last five years.
