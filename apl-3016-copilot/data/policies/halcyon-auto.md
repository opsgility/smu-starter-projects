# Halcyon Insurance - Standard Personal Auto Policy (PAP)

**Document ID:** halcyon-pap-2026
**Line of business:** Personal Auto
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## Liability

Bodily injury and property damage liability limits are stated on the
declarations page. Halcyon writes split limits (for example 100/300/100) and
combined single limits.

## Collision and comprehensive

Collision and Comprehensive (Other Than Collision) are optional coverages,
each subject to the deductible shown on the declarations page. Comprehensive
covers hail, falling objects, animal strike, theft, and glass damage.

## Rental reimbursement

Rental Reimbursement is available by endorsement (Form HA-22) and pays up to
the daily and per-loss limits shown on the endorsement while the insured
vehicle is out of service due to a covered loss.

## Exclusions

Halcyon PAP excludes damage from racing, intentional acts, and use of the
vehicle for delivery or transportation network services (rideshare) unless
the Rideshare endorsement (Form HA-31) is attached.
