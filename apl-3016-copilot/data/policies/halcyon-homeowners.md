# Halcyon Insurance - Standard Homeowners Policy (HO-3)

**Document ID:** halcyon-ho3-2026
**Line of business:** Homeowners
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## Dwelling and other structures

Halcyon HO-3 covers the residence dwelling on an open-perils basis and other
structures on the premises (detached garage, storage shed, fence) up to ten
percent of the dwelling limit. Wind and hail damage to the dwelling and other
structures is covered subject to the policy deductible.

## Personal property

Personal property is covered on a named-perils basis for the sixteen HO-3
perils, including fire, lightning, windstorm, theft, and water discharge from
plumbing. Jewelry, firearms, and electronics have sub-limits - see the
declarations page.

## Water backup

Sewer and drain water backup is **not** included in the base HO-3 form.
Halcyon offers a Water Backup endorsement (Form HE-14) that adds coverage up
to the endorsement limit.

## Exclusions

Flood, earth movement, and normal wear and tear are excluded. Flood coverage
must be written through a separate NFIP or Halcyon Private Flood policy.
