# Halcyon Insurance - Standard Personal Umbrella Policy (PUP)

**Document ID:** halcyon-pup-2026
**Line of business:** Personal Umbrella
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## What the umbrella covers

The Halcyon Personal Umbrella provides an extra layer of liability protection
above the required underlying limits on the insured's Halcyon homeowners,
auto, and watercraft policies. Coverage attaches once the underlying limits
are exhausted.

## Underlying limit requirements

The umbrella requires minimum underlying limits on each covered exposure.
Auto liability must be at least 250/500/100 (or a combined single limit of
$300,000), and homeowners liability must be at least $300,000. If the
underlying limits fall below these, the insured is responsible for the gap.

## Worldwide coverage

Halcyon PUP applies worldwide for bodily injury and property damage caused by
an occurrence during the policy period. Suits must be brought in the United
States, its territories, or Canada.

## Exclusions

The umbrella excludes business pursuits, professional services, intentional
acts, and any exposure that would not be covered by the underlying policy.
