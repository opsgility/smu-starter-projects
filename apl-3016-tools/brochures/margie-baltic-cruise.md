# Margie's Baltic Capitals Cruise — 10 Days, Copenhagen to Stockholm

## The trip

Sail the northern Baltic from Copenhagen to Stockholm across ten days aboard the mid-size *M/S Nordlys*. Ten days, seven ports, four countries — Copenhagen, Warnemünde (with an included day trip to Berlin), Gdańsk, Klaipėda, Riga, Tallinn, Helsinki, and a scenic overnight sail into Stockholm to conclude. Every port includes a Margie's-guided walking tour, and every day at sea includes an onboard lecture from a regional historian.

## Who this trip is for

Travelers who want a low-decision-fatigue way to see the Nordic and Baltic capitals in one arc — you unpack once and let the ship handle the transfers. Well-suited for repeat cruisers who have already done Norwegian fjords and are looking for the next Nordic chapter, and for first-time cruisers who prefer smaller, historian-guided vessels over the mega-ship experience. Not recommended for travelers whose primary interest is beach time or nightlife — this is a museum-and-old-town itinerary.

## Included excursions

Each port includes a two-to-three-hour Margie's-guided walking tour led by a licensed local guide. In Warnemünde, the included day trip runs from Warnemünde station into Berlin's Hauptbahnhof via ICE, with four hours of guided time around the Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag exterior, and Museum Island before returning the same day. In Tallinn, the walking tour ends at the Kadriorg Palace grounds; travelers who purchase the optional palace-interior upgrade in advance are handed off to Kadriorg's own docent for the interior tour.

## Optional add-ons (must be booked 45+ days before sailing)

Riga Art Nouveau extended architecture walk (3 hours), Helsinki design-district workshop with a Marimekko textile designer (half day), Stockholm archipelago day-trip by traditional sailboat (full day, adds one hotel night in Stockholm). Availability is not guaranteed at the port — bookings inside 45 days incur a 25% late-booking surcharge and are subject to guide availability.

## What's included in the base fare

Cabin (Category B outside stateroom or upgrade), all shipboard meals excluding specialty restaurants, all included excursions listed above, all port taxes and gratuities, unlimited non-premium wifi on the ship, and round-trip transfers between the ship and Copenhagen airport / Stockholm Arlanda airport on embarkation and debarkation days when Margie's books your international flights.

## What's NOT included

Airfare to Copenhagen and from Stockholm (Margie's can book at a bundled rate), pre-cruise or post-cruise hotel nights (recommended: one night pre-cruise in Copenhagen to avoid same-day arrival stress), travel insurance (mandatory — Margie's partners with a broker or you can bring your own proof of coverage), premium wifi upgrade, specialty restaurant covers, spa services, and any optional add-on excursions listed above.

## Cancellation policy

Cancellations more than 90 days before sailing are refunded in full minus a $250 per-person administrative fee. Cancellations 61-90 days before sailing forfeit 25% of the base fare. Cancellations 31-60 days before sailing forfeit 50%. Cancellations 30 days or fewer before sailing forfeit 100% and can only be recovered through the travel insurance policy — no exceptions. Name changes on a booked cabin are permitted up to 45 days before sailing for a $150 per-person administrative fee; after 45 days, name changes are treated as a cancellation of the original guest plus a new booking at then-prevailing rates.

## When to sail

The core season is May through mid-September. Late-May and early-September sailings enjoy softer light and thinner port crowds; July and early August are the warmest and busiest. Margie's does not sell this itinerary October through April — Baltic weather turns and several ports scale down their guided-tour operations.
