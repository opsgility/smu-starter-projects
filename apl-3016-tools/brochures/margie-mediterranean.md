# Margie's Mediterranean Passage — 12 Days, Barcelona to Athens

## The trip

A twelve-day land-and-sea Mediterranean itinerary that pairs three land nights in Barcelona with a nine-night eastbound cruise that finishes in Athens. Ports of call: Palma de Mallorca, Ajaccio (Corsica), Rome (Civitavecchia), Naples with an included Pompeii excursion, Valletta (Malta), Katakolon with an included Olympia archaeological site excursion, Santorini, Mykonos, and Piraeus for the finale into Athens. Land portion in Barcelona is a fully-guided small-group experience; sea portion is aboard the boutique *M/S Ligure*.

## Who this trip is for

Travelers who want the two most-requested Mediterranean experiences — Barcelona's Gaudí architecture and the Greek Islands — stitched together without the airport churn of separate bookings. Excellent for anniversaries and milestone birthdays; strong for multi-generational family groups where grandparents want the cultural depth and grandchildren want the beaches. Not recommended for travelers who need pool-days-only vacations — every port has a curated cultural component and the Barcelona land days are on-foot walking tours.

## Included excursions

**Barcelona (3 land days):** guided Sagrada Família interior tour with priority entry, guided Park Güell tour, guided walking tour of the Gothic Quarter and Born neighborhoods including a tapas lunch stop. **At sea:** the included excursions vary by port — Pompeii (guided archaeological tour from Naples), Olympia (guided archaeological tour from Katakolon), Valletta (guided walking tour of the fortified old town with palace grounds access), Santorini (Oia sunset viewing with reserved space), Athens (Acropolis + Acropolis Museum full-day guided).

## Optional add-ons (must be booked 45+ days before sailing)

Mallorca cave-swim excursion (half day), Corsica scenic hike on the Sentier des Douaniers (half day, moderate difficulty), Rome private-driver full-day return to Vatican City and Colosseum (full day, adds significant walking), Mykonos catamaran sunset sail (evening, weather-dependent), Athens Cape Sounion sunset day-trip (afternoon, adds one hotel night in Athens). Availability is not guaranteed at the port; late bookings inside 45 days incur a 25% surcharge and are subject to guide availability.

## What's included in the base fare

Three hotel nights in Barcelona (4-star, breakfast included), transfer to embarkation port on Day 4, cabin aboard *M/S Ligure* (Category B outside stateroom or upgrade), all shipboard meals excluding specialty restaurants, all included excursions listed above, all port taxes and gratuities, unlimited non-premium wifi on the ship, transfer from Piraeus to a central Athens hotel on debarkation day and one Athens hotel night to catch your international flight home the following day.

## What's NOT included

International airfare to Barcelona and from Athens, pre-cruise Barcelona hotel nights beyond the included three, travel insurance (mandatory), premium wifi upgrade, specialty restaurant covers, spa services, wine or bar tabs beyond the included dinner wines on the ship, and any optional add-on excursions listed above.

## Cancellation policy

Cancellations more than 90 days before departure are refunded in full minus a $350 per-person administrative fee (higher than the Baltic itinerary because the Barcelona hotel block is prepaid at booking). Cancellations 61-90 days out forfeit 30%. Cancellations 31-60 days out forfeit 60%. Cancellations 30 days or fewer forfeit 100% and can only be recovered through the travel insurance policy. Name changes are permitted up to 45 days before departure for a $200 per-person administrative fee; after 45 days, name changes are treated as a cancellation plus a new booking at then-prevailing rates.

## When to sail

The itinerary runs late April through late October. Shoulder weeks (late April, early May, late September, early October) offer the most comfortable temperatures for the walking-heavy Barcelona and Rome portions. Peak summer (mid-June through late August) delivers the beach-and-swim moments in Santorini and Mykonos but adds meaningful heat to the land portion — Margie's flags this at booking for guests over 65 or with mobility concerns.
