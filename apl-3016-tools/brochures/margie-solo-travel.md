# Margie's Solo Traveler Collection — Trip Design for the Single Guest

## The philosophy

Margie's Solo Traveler Collection is a set of itineraries hand-designed for travelers going alone — not just tolerating a solo guest on a family-oriented trip, but built around the specific needs a solo traveler names on their intake call: safety, easy connection with other solo guests when they want it, quiet time when they don't, and pricing that doesn't punish them with a 100% single supplement. Every trip in the Collection carries a single-supplement cap of 25% over the per-person double-occupancy rate.

## Who these trips are for

First-time solo travelers who want a soft-launch — a fully-planned itinerary with a local guide the whole time and a small group of fellow solo travelers to sit with at dinner without being obligated to. Also excellent for experienced solo travelers who are tired of assembling logistics themselves and want a trusted operator to handle the transfers, guides, and reservations so they can just walk out of the hotel and go.

Not the right fit for travelers who want a fully independent unguided experience (Margie's Independent Design service is a better fit — different intake, different pricing), or for travelers whose primary goal is to meet a romantic partner (there are operators who specialize in that; Margie's does not).

## Trips currently in the Collection

**Kyoto in autumn — 8 days.** Four hotel nights in central Kyoto (Nishijin district), three in Hakone. Local guide for the first 3 days in Kyoto covering Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama bamboo, Kinkaku-ji, and a hands-on tea-ceremony workshop; solo time on days 4-5; guided Hakone day for the *Owakudani* geothermal valley and the *Amazake-chaya* tea-house forest hike. Best October and early November for autumn color.

**Iceland ring road — 10 days.** Small-group van tour (max 8 guests plus 2 guides) circumnavigating the country counter-clockwise from Reykjavík. Includes glacier hike with certified guide, Vatnajökull ice-cave tour, Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon boat, whale watching from Húsavík, geothermal spa evenings at Lake Mývatn and Krauma. Best May through September (September for northern-lights viewing).

**Portuguese coast walking tour — 7 days.** Guided coastal walking from Cascais to Ericeira with hotel-to-hotel luggage transfers; 4-8 miles per day at your own pace with the guide sweeping back and forth along the trail. Small group (max 12), often 5-6 solo travelers. Every dinner is included and eaten together as a group at pre-arranged restaurants. Best March through May and September through November — summer inland heat makes the coastal walking uncomfortable.

## What's included on every Collection trip

All in-country transfers, all listed hotel nights (single-occupancy rooms — never a shared or twin default), all guided excursions listed in the trip page, all group meals, 24/7 access to a Margie's WhatsApp support number staffed in the traveler's home time zone, and a pre-trip video call with the local lead guide two weeks before departure.

## What's NOT included

International airfare (Margie's can book bundled), travel insurance (mandatory), meals not called out as "group meal" or "included" on the day-by-day, alcohol at group meals beyond the wine pour provided, tips for guides (a suggested amount is provided in the pre-trip document), and any optional excursion the traveler chooses to add on the trip page.

## Solo-specific policies

**Room type:** every Collection trip books single-occupancy hotel rooms as standard. Margie's does not offer a "room-share to reduce cost" option on Collection trips — the point of the Collection is that solos are not price-penalized.

**Single supplement:** capped at 25% over the per-person double-occupancy rate on every Collection trip. This is Margie's absorbing part of the true hotel single-supplement cost; the flat 25% is why Margie's cannot discount Collection trips further.

## Cancellation policy

Cancellations more than 90 days before departure are refunded in full minus a $250 per-person administrative fee. Cancellations 61-90 days out forfeit 25%. Cancellations 31-60 days out forfeit 60%. Cancellations 30 days or fewer forfeit 100% and can only be recovered through the travel insurance policy. Because Collection trips are booked as single-occupancy from the start, name changes are treated as a cancellation of the original guest plus a new booking at then-prevailing rates — there is no room-mate substitution option.

## Bookings and intake

Every Collection booking begins with a 30-minute intake call with a Margie's solo-travel specialist — no exceptions, including for repeat Margie's guests. The intake exists so the specialist can flag any accessibility, dietary, or interpersonal preferences (e.g. "I prefer not to share meals with children under 12") to the local lead guide before the trip.
